3-Step Quick Setup

Works on any Android 8.0+ phone — no root or developer mode needed.

1

Download the APK

Tap the download button. If prompted to allow unknown apps, tap Settings → Allow from this source.

2

Grant System Access

Open the Access tab in Syncly and enable Notification Listener and Calendar Read & Write until both show ✔ Granted.

3

Pick Your Calendar Account

Tap the top-right account chip on the Home screen to select your preferred student or personal Google Calendar email.

  1. Open your phone's Settings → Apps → Syncly (or tap the 1-tap shortcut inside Syncly).
  2. Tap the ⋮ (three dots icon) in the top-right corner of the App Info screen.
  3. Tap "Allow restricted settings" and verify with your fingerprint or PIN.
  4. Return to Syncly's Access tab and toggle on Notification Listener Access!

Latest Release

Instant Background Calendar Sync & Strict Casual Chat Filtering

🗓 October 2026
  • ✓ Instant Background Calendar Sync: Integrated READ_SYNC_SETTINGS and WRITE_SYNC_SETTINGS permissions to guarantee immediate Google Calendar synchronization without requiring users to manually refresh accounts in phone settings.
  • ✓ Expedited Dual Authority Sync: Enforces automated master sync and triggers expedited refresh on both CalendarContract.AUTHORITY and com.google.android.calendar.
  • ✓ Fixed Calendar Provider Exception: Eliminated restricted VISIBLE column writes that caused calendar insertion failures on select Android versions.
  • ✓ Strict Casual Chat Filtering: Suppresses casual questions, greetings, and conversational chatter from being parsed into calendar events.
  • ✓ Source Whitelist Enforcement: Completely removed and excluded non-official sources (such as Aakhari Prayatnam).
